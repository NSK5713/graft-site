Graft

Shift pay, on your iPhone.

Graft records the shifts you work and shows an estimate of what you’ll take home. Your jobs, shifts and pay stay on the phone.

Privacy Policy Terms of Use Contact Subscriptions

What Graft does

  • Log shifts across more than one job, including overnight shifts and breaks.
  • Clock in and out, and see hours for the current week and pay period.
  • Estimate take-home pay after the tax, National Insurance and pension settings on each job. The figures are estimates, not a payslip.
  • Optional reminders, an optional workplace pin, and an optional copy in your own iCloud.

Graft Premium (unlimited jobs and templates, repeating shifts, analytics, calendar sync, sharing and custom colours) is an optional auto-renewable subscription billed by Apple. Monthly is £2.99 with no free trial. Yearly is £19.99, and new subscribers get one month free — save 44% versus twelve months at £2.99 (£35.88).

Subscriptions

Where to subscribe

Open Graft, sign in, then go to Settings → Account. Monthly is £2.99 with no free trial. Yearly is one month free for a new subscriber, then £19.99 a year (save 44% versus paying monthly). The buttons show the price from the App Store, with Subscribe and Restore purchases.

How to restore

On the same iPhone Apple ID that bought Premium, open Settings → Account → Restore purchases. Restoring puts Premium on the Graft account you are signed in to.

How to cancel

Graft cannot cancel an Apple subscription from inside the app. Apple bills it and Apple cancels it.

  1. Open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions, or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  2. Choose Graft Premium and cancel. You can also open Settings → Account → Manage subscription in Graft while Premium is active.

The yearly free month converts to £19.99 a year unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. After that, and for monthly, cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to stop the next renewal. Premium stays available until the time you already have ends. Deleting the app, or deleting your Graft account, does not cancel the subscription.

Payment, renewals and refunds are handled by Apple under the Apple Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

FAQ

Is my rota on a Graft server?

No. Jobs, shifts and pay are stored on the device. Sign-in (name and email) is handled by Clerk. An iCloud backup, if you turn it on, goes to your own iCloud account. See the Privacy Policy.

Why does the pay estimate differ from my payslip?

Graft does not know your tax code, student loan, or every deduction your employer applies. Compare the estimate with your payslip.

I reinstalled and my shifts are gone.

Without iCloud backup, a reinstall starts empty. Turn on backup under Settings → Backup, privacy & data before you delete the app if you want a copy in iCloud. You can also export a backup file or a CSV from that screen.

The widget says nothing is scheduled.

Open Graft once after installing so it can share today’s shift with the Home Screen widget. The widget shows the next shift and this week’s hours from the data already on the phone.
