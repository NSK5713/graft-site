Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026. This page describes what the iOS app does with data today.

Who to contact

Email nskhuenkhoksung@live.com. The person providing Graft is based in the Isle of Man. Graft does not run its own server for your shifts or pay.

Account

App Store and TestFlight builds sign you in with Clerk. Clerk stores your name, email address, and either your password (Graft never sees it) or the link to the Google or Apple account you used. Clerk also keeps the session records it needs to sign you in.

Jobs, shifts, pay rates and payslip comparisons are not sent to Clerk. Settings → Account → Delete account deletes the Clerk account and the data on that device, and the iCloud copy if you had backup on.

The web app, and mobile builds that are not store builds, can use an account that exists only on that device. Those passwords are stored as a salted hash, not in plaintext.

On this iPhone

Unless you turn on iCloud backup, the following stays on the device:

  • Jobs: workplace, role, colour, hourly rate, overtime, holiday pay, pay cycle, pension and the tax settings you enter.
  • Shifts: dates, times, breaks and whether a shift is worked or scheduled.
  • A workplace pin, if you set one. Graft keeps no location history.
  • Preferences, including reminders and whether Premium is active on this account.

iCloud backup

Backup is off until you turn it on under Settings → Backup, privacy & data. When it is on, one copy of your Graft data (jobs, shifts, templates, clock, preferences and payslip comparisons, including a workplace pin) is saved with Apple’s iCloud key-value storage under your Apple ID. Graft cannot read that copy. Turning backup off stops saving. Deleting the account removes the copy.

Subscriptions

Apple processes Premium payments. Graft reads the subscription status on the device. Graft does not receive your card number or Apple ID payment details.

Location and workplace search

“When in use” location lets you pin a workplace. “Always” is requested only if you turn on the arrival reminder. Graft never clocks you in by itself and does not keep a trail of where you have been.

If you search for a workplace, the text you type is sent to Photon (photon.komoot.io) and, if needed, OpenStreetMap Nominatim, to find matching places. If location permission is already granted, the search also sends a latitude and longitude so results are near you. The search does not include your Graft account, name or email. The request identifies itself as Graft.

Other device features

  • Notifications are scheduled on the phone. Graft does not register a remote push token.
  • Calendar sync writes the job name, times and workplace into the calendar you pick. It does not write your pay.
  • Update checks may contact Expo at u.expo.dev with the platform, runtime version, release channel and update id. That check does not include your name, email, shifts or pay.
  • Crashes: the copy Graft keeps is on the device and is not sent to Graft. TestFlight may send crash logs to Apple under Apple’s own policy.

There is no advertising and no analytics SDK. The Analytics screen calculates figures on the phone from your shifts.

Children

Graft is not directed at children. It is a tool for people tracking paid work.

Retention

Data on the device is removed when you delete the account or the app. An iCloud backup remains until you delete the account or turn backup off and the copy expires with Apple. Clerk’s retention of sign-in records after deletion follows Clerk’s own terms.

To ask about the name and email held for your account, email nskhuenkhoksung@live.com or delete the account in the app.
