Estimates, not a payslip

Pay figures are estimates for your own planning. They are not a payslip, not payroll, and not tax or financial advice. Graft does not apply a tax code or student loan. Illustrative UK PAYE and Isle of Man ITIP are used only when that job’s country is the UK or the Isle of Man. Everywhere else, Graft uses the percentages you enter. Your payslip is the source of truth. Do not rely on Graft for a tax or legal decision.

Your data

Jobs, shifts and pay are stored on your device. Store builds sign in with Clerk, which holds your name and email, not your rota. iCloud backup is optional and off by default; the copy sits in your own iCloud account. The Privacy Policy has the detail. You can export a backup or a CSV from Settings.

Graft Premium

Premium is optional. On the App Store it is an auto-renewable subscription sold by Apple:

  • Monthly — the monthly price shown by the App Store in your country (uk.graft.app.premium.monthly). No free trial.
  • Yearly — the yearly price shown by the App Store in your country (uk.graft.app.premium.yearly). New subscribers get one month free, then that yearly price. The introductory offer is one per person for this subscription group. If yearly costs less than twelve monthly payments, the app shows the saving from the prices Apple returned.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase, at the price the App Store shows. The yearly free month converts to the yearly price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. The subscription then renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period. You can manage or cancel in your App Store account settings after purchase. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription that replaces it.

Subscribe and restore from Settings → Account in the app. Cancelling is done by Apple, not by deleting Graft. How to cancel and restore is on the support page.

Outside the App Store (the web app, and non-store builds), the Premium switch is a local feature flag with no payment.
